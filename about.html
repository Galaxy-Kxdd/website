Our Story

About Blommas

Named for the Swedish word for "flowers," Blommas was built on one idea: a night out should feel like something is blooming.

How We Started

From a shared love of good food and better drinks.

Blommas opened its doors as a neighborhood cocktail bar and eatery for people who want both done well — not one at the expense of the other. Our kitchen builds seasonal, produce-forward small plates, while our bar team crafts cocktails with the same care, using fresh-pressed juices, house syrups and small-batch spirits.

The space is designed to feel warm and a little romantic, with soft lighting, botanical touches and a room that hums at every hour of the evening.
